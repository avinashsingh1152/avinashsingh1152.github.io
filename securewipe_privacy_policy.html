Welcome to the Privacy Policy for SecureWipe. SecureWipe is designed as a powerful privacy tool available 100% free of charge for all Android users globally. There are no hidden fees, premium tiers, or subscriptions.

2. Data Collection and Usage (Zero Data Policy)

SecureWipe operates completely locally on your Android device. It uses algorithms to systematically overwrite your device's storage with multiple passes of binary data to ensure previously deleted files, app records, and hidden folders are entirely unrecoverable.

  • No External Servers: We do not upload, transmit, or back up any of your data to external servers or cloud services.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The app does not integrate any third-party tracking, telemetry, or behavioral analytics software.
  • No Advertising SDKs: Because the app is completely free, we do not monetize via ads. Therefore, no advertising networks are tracking your usage.

3. Required Device Permissions

To function securely and successfully overwrite all available space on your device, SecureWipe requires specific permissions upon launch. These permissions are used strictly for local data erasure:

  • All Files Access / Full Storage Permission: Required to access your Android device's internal storage (and external SD card, if applicable) to write random binary data (`0`s and `1`s) until memory is full, and to permanently delete residual application data and hidden files.
  • Administrator / Root Access (Optional/Conditional): To thoroughly wipe locked system caches and deep application data across all apps, SecureWipe may request elevated privileges. This is exclusively used to perform the data wiping process.
